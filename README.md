# Pulse Metronome 

An ultra-loud, minimalist metronome designed specifically for guitarists.

![Pulse Metronome Preview](media/readme%20screenshot%20.png)

---

## 🎸 Why Use Pulse?

* **Super Loud**: Easily hear the beat even while playing along with loud instruments or amplifiers.
* **Popular Time Signatures**: Includes all common time signatures (`2/4`, `3/4`, `4/4`, `5/4`, `6/8`) with an automatic downbeat accent.
* **Multiple Click Sounds**: Choose between different sounds including sharp click, wood block, cowbell, and hand clap.
* **Zero Distractions, Single-Viewport Stage**: No ads, no scrolling, and no clutter—just a clean, full-screen stage.
* **Very Precise Timing**: Rock-solid, highly accurate timing that never lags or drifts off beat.

---

## ⚡ Shortcuts
* **Spacebar** — Start / Stop Metronome
* **H** — Toggle Control Sidebar
* **↑ / ↓** — Adjust BPM by 1 (Hold **Shift** for ±5)
